TimelyInsights

Ask the organization. Know by lunch.

TimelyInsights is a decision support platform. Field a survey across your people, customers, or members — and get the analysis back while the decision is still open.

At a glance

  • InputThe decision you're facing, described in plain language
  • OutputAnalysed results and a written recommendation with evidence attached
  • Built forAnyone who has to decide on behalf of a group
  • Not forTurning a self-selected sample into a representative one

The problem

The question that would settle the argument never gets asked.

Asking your organization something properly has always been expensive. Writing a decent instrument takes expertise most teams don't have, fielding it takes weeks, and analysing open-text answers takes someone reading hundreds of comments and forming a view.

So the question doesn't get asked. The decision gets made from the loudest voice in the room, or from the three people who happened to reply to an email — and everyone privately knows the evidence was thin.

TimelyInsights compresses that loop to the point where asking is cheaper than guessing.

The loop

Question to answer, without the research project.

01

Ask

Describe the decision you're facing. The platform drafts the instrument — questions, scales, and logic — and you edit rather than start from blank.

02

Field

Send it to the group whose answer matters: staff, leadership, customers, members. Responses land as they arrive.

03

Decide

Results are analyzed on arrival — themes named, segments split, disagreement surfaced — and written up as a recommendation you can act on.

What makes it fast

Open text, actually read

Free-text answers are the ones that matter and the ones nobody reads. Every comment is coded, clustered, and quoted back with its theme.

Disagreement, not just averages

A 50/50 split and a lukewarm consensus produce the same mean. The platform tells you which one you have.

Segments that explain themselves

Where two groups diverge, you get the cut and the reason — not a filter panel to go hunting through.

A written recommendation

Every round ends in prose a leadership team can read: what was asked, what came back, what it implies.

Repeatable rounds

Ask the same question next quarter and the movement is the finding. Instruments and cohorts are reusable by design.

Evidence kept attached

Every claim in the write-up traces to the responses behind it. Nothing is asserted without the receipts.

Who it's for

Anyone who has to decide on behalf of a group.

Leadership teams

Before you commit to the reorg, the policy, or the price change — find out what the people affected actually think.

Membership organizations

Associations, boards, and co-ops that owe their members a say and need it documented.

Operators with no analyst

You have the questions and the audience. What you don't have is a research function.

Being straight with you

What it won't do.

It won't make a badly framed question meaningful. If the decision is vague, the responses will be too — which is why the platform pushes you to state the decision before it drafts anything.

It won't turn a small or self-selected sample into a representative one. It tells you who answered and who didn't, and treats that as part of the finding rather than a footnote.

And it won't make the decision. It gets you to a defensible view quickly, with the evidence attached; the call is still yours.
