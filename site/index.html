How we work

An AI technology company, not an agency.

We sell software, not hours. The consulting we do exists to sharpen the products — which means the incentives point at making the software good rather than at keeping people busy.

Products before hours

Engagements are limited and product-led. If the software can solve it, that's the answer we'd rather give.

Evidence stays attached

Every output traces to its source — the conversation, the responses, the step. Claims you can't audit don't ship.

Tested on real work

We run our own tools on our own operations first. Demos on clean data prove nothing about messy reality.

Honest about limits

AI fails in specific, knowable ways. We'd rather name them than let you find them in front of your team.
